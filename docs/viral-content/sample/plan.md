**Idea:** a countdown reveal. She walks past Madeira's famous spots, dismissing each ("it's not…"), and the payoff
is one place viewers haven't seen. Learns from Kara and Nate's island estate reel (6.9M views, 52x their usual):
an open loop in line one, the obvious highlights rejected one by one, the payoff held to the end.
**Format:** reveal tour, voice-over + word-by-word captions. **Length:** 32 s (board median for voice tours is 44 to
54 s; she's a smaller account, so shorter keeps completion up). **Pace:** about 2.5 cuts per 10 s, every cut a
new place.
